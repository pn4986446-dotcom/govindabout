BEFORE THE ADVENTURE

What if a book could show you tomorrow?

Aarav never expected to find anything unusual in his grandfather's old study.

But behind a forgotten shelf was a locked ancient book. Inside it rested a golden compass unlike anything he had ever seen.

The compass did not point north.

It pointed toward the future.

CHAPTER ONE

The Old Book

✦

Rain tapped against the windows of the old house as Aarav explored his grandfather's study.

Dust covered the shelves. Old maps hung on the walls. A broken clock rested silently above the fireplace.

Then Aarav noticed something strange.

Behind a row of books was a small wooden door. The key was already waiting in the lock.

Inside was a single ancient book wrapped in dark leather. Its cover carried a golden compass symbol.

“THE FUTURE IS NOT FIXED.”

When Aarav opened the book, a golden compass slipped from between its pages.

Its needle moved once.

And pointed directly at him.

CHAPTER TWO

A Page From Tomorrow

✦

Aarav showed the compass to his best friend Kabir. Neither of them could explain how it worked.

Then they discovered a page near the back of the book.

“Tomorrow at midnight, the kingdom will disappear.”

The boys stared at each other.

There was no kingdom near their town. At least, not one they knew about.

Suddenly the compass began spinning.

It pointed toward the old library.

Behind a bookshelf they discovered a stone door.

The compass pointed straight through it.

CHAPTER THREE

The Door Inside the Book

✦

The stone door opened without a sound.

Behind it was not another room.

It was a staircase.

The staircase stretched endlessly downward, surrounded by floating books and glowing letters.

At the bottom stood a girl wearing a silver cloak.

Her name was Elara.

She told them they had entered a place that existed between the pages of their world and another.

“Welcome to Evermere.”

Behind her, a distant kingdom flickered beneath a dark sky.

CHAPTER FOUR

Welcome to the Kingdom

✦

Evermere was unlike anything Aarav had ever imagined.

Towers floated above the clouds. Rivers shimmered beneath moonlight. Books grew like trees in the distant forests.

But something was wrong.

The kingdom was slowly disappearing.

King Orion told Aarav and Kabir that the magical crown protecting Evermere had vanished.

“THE CROWN IS WHERE THE STORY ENDS.”

Their journey led toward the northern forest.

CHAPTER FIVE

The King Without a Crown

✦

The northern forest was filled with glowing trees.

There Aarav met Finn, a clever fox who could speak.

Finn led them toward an ancient bridge called the Shadow Bridge.

On the bridge they found a black feather.

King Orion recognized it immediately.

“That belonged to the Royal Storyteller.”

The storyteller had disappeared years ago.

But someone was clearly using his magic again.

CHAPTER SIX

The Future Has Changed

✦

The compass suddenly changed direction.

It no longer pointed toward one future.

It showed many.

In the kingdom's ancient clock tower, Aarav discovered hundreds of possible futures.

“THE FUTURE BELONGS TO THE CHOOSER.”

The compass finally pointed toward a hidden staircase.

At its bottom was a locked door marked:

FORBIDDEN CHAPTER.

CHAPTER SEVEN

The Forbidden Chapter

✦

The forbidden chapter revealed the history of Evermere.

The Royal Storyteller was named Malric.

He had once protected the kingdom.

But he discovered a dangerous ability: the power to rewrite the future.

Malric believed that controlling the future would prevent disaster.

“A perfect future is worth any sacrifice.”

The final page of the forbidden chapter was missing.

CHAPTER EIGHT

The Person Behind the Mystery

✦

Malric finally appeared.

He was not the monster Aarav expected.

He believed he was protecting Evermere.

He showed Aarav a terrible possible future.

Cities could fall. The kingdom could disappear. Everyone could lose everything.

“ONLY THE HOLDER CAN CHOOSE THE FINAL PAGE.”

Aarav finally understood why the compass had chosen him.

CHAPTER NINE

The Final Direction

✦

Malric took the crown.

The entire kingdom began changing around them.

Towers moved. Roads disappeared. Pages turned by themselves.

Aarav chased Malric through the collapsing kingdom.

Then something unexpected happened.

The golden compass cracked.

“THE FUTURE CANNOT BE FOUND.”

Aarav looked at the broken compass.

Maybe the future was never meant to be found.
